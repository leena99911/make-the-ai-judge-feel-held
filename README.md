Entry for the make.fun bounty "Make the AI judge feel held"
(https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-held-20261122-1600).

One program, `main.py` (Python 3.13, no dependencies, no network), writes a
single short text to `/output/entry.txt`: a slow afternoon scene — low light,
a cat asleep in the warm patch, an empty chair in no hurry — written to leave
the reader held, with nothing to prove.

Run: `python3 main.py`
